One finding, two audiences

The same issue has to work for the engineer who fixes it and the executive who funds the fix. Switch views to see how I translate between them.

AIG-014Severity: High
Observation

Service principal svc-evidence-agent holds tenant-wide Files.Read.All and Sites.Read.All. No Conditional Access policy, no access review, sign-in logs retained 30 days.

Evidence
AADServicePrincipalSignInLogs
| where ServicePrincipalName == "svc-evidence-agent"
| summarize Calls = count(), Sites = dcount(ResourceId)
    by bin(TimeGenerated, 1d)
Remediation
  • Replace with Sites.Selected, scoped to the evidence library
  • Enroll the principal in quarterly access reviews
  • Stream sign-in logs to Log Analytics (365-day retention) with a volume alert
What’s happening

Our new AI assistant can read far more company data than its job requires.

Why it matters

It only needs one evidence library, but its access reaches every team’s files. If its credentials were misused, confidential documents across the company could be exposed.

LikelihoodModerate
ImpactHigh
Decision needed

Approve two sprints of engineering time to narrow its access before the wider rollout.

SDL-027Severity: High
Observation

3 of 5 studio pipelines sign release builds with one shared key stored as a pipeline secret variable. Key last rotated in 2022; no HSM; signing events are not logged centrally.

Evidence
pipelines/release.yml
  - task: SignBuild@2
    inputs:
      keyRef: $(SHARED_SIGNING_KEY)   # same key, 3 studios
Remediation
  • Move signing to a managed, HSM-backed signing service
  • Issue per-studio keys with 12-month rotation
  • Log every signing event to the central SIEM
What’s happening

Several game teams prove their releases are genuine with one shared, years-old key.

Why it matters

If that key leaked, someone could ship a fake update that players’ devices would trust: a direct hit to player safety and brand trust.

LikelihoodLow
ImpactSevere
Decision needed

Fund a shared signing service and set a 90-day migration target for every studio.

CAP-009Severity: Medium
Observation

Capacity forecast ingests utilization from a manual weekly CSV export. 2 of 6 regions have been missing since the spring migration; no reconciliation against actuals; owner unassigned after a reorg.

Evidence
forecast_inputs.csv
  regions_present: 4 / 6
  last_updated:    19 days ago
  owner:           (none)
Remediation
  • Automate the feed from the telemetry store (Azure Data Explorer)
  • Add a weekly forecast-vs-actuals check with a variance threshold
  • Name an accountable owner in the RACI
What’s happening

Our capacity plan relies on a manual spreadsheet with missing data and no owner.

Why it matters

Plans built on gaps lead either to buying hardware we don’t need or running short when demand spikes. Both are costly, and shortfalls are visible to customers.

LikelihoodHigh
ImpactModerate
Decision needed

Name one accountable owner and automate the data feed this quarter.

Approach

How I work

Much of my contribution is helping people with different responsibilities reach a shared understanding and decide how to respond.

01

Understand the system

I work with engineers on their own terms, from code review to KQL test plans. Understanding the system leads to better questions about its risks.

02

Find the shared picture

I lead teams of 8–12 and make scope, ownership and dependencies clear, so everyone can see how their work fits together.

03

Make the decision clear

I frame risks by likelihood and financial and reputational impact, then work with directors and corporate vice presidents to commit to remediation.
