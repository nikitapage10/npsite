01 / Range

I’ve worked in more rooms than most.

Banks, insurers, fashion, beauty, grocery, telecom, and now LinkedIn, Xbox, and Copilot. Ten years across eight industries taught me that the problems rhyme even when the vocabulary doesn’t. That’s what lets me walk into a new domain and see the whole picture quickly.

Three studios, one baseline

After Microsoft’s $75.4B Activision Blizzard acquisition, I worked with security and engineering leaders across ABK, ZeniMax, and Minecraft. I adapted to each studio’s rhythm and wrote the landscape assessment the next phase of planning was built on.

Cloud to custom silicon

Cloud, identity, privacy, AI, secure development, supply chain, custom-silicon R&D, and datacenter energy and capacity. That’s more than ten domains, and in each one I learned to talk with engineers on their own terms.

02 / Translation

I speak business to tech, and tech to business.

Engineers want precision. Executives want a decision. I work between them, aligning dozens of engineering and business stakeholders and turning a technical problem into a business case: how likely it is, and what it could cost. Then I get directors and corporate vice presidents to commit.

Two acquisitions, one migration plan

When services from two acquisitions moved to Azure, I built the planning matrix from scratch, ran weekly technical learning sessions, and aligned a senior executive on who owns what and when each piece is ready.

Making AI make sense to 200 people

I led AI enablement for a department of about 200 professionals, showing them how to apply AI to their everyday work. I directed 13 contributors and built the demo chatbots myself.

03 / Building

I don’t just advise. I build.

A high-level view only helps if it reaches the ground. So I build the tools: AI workflows, dashboards, and prototypes. Through my own company, Nikita Page Productions, I help founders turn early ideas into plans they can actually build.

An AI hub that keeps the story current

Built in Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry, and Azure. It connects evidence to risks, keeps the narrative up to date on its own, and limits each agent’s access to what data-loss-prevention rules allow.

From idea to buildable plan

With startups and small businesses I lead discovery, workflow design, and prototyping, along with technology strategy and architecture.
